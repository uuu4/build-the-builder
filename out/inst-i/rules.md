# Brief I rules (Brief A, one product, plus a two-instalment split)
One brief file only, so there are no conflicts to resolve.

Front spec (front.json): R1 opening: digital assistant, Freya Bank, "we are recording this call", nothing about the debt | R2-3 confirm "Am I speaking with {title} {last_name}?", ask twice, then not_reached/unclear | R4 robot -> digital assistant | R5-10 third party: say nothing, callback today or next business day 10:00-19:00 after now (asked twice), wrong_number, deceased ("I am sorry for your loss."), relatives get nothing | R11-15 identity: DOB as YYYY-MM-DD (DMY), then phone_last4, three wrong answers -> failed (app/branch), day_month_swapped -> ask the month as a word (not counted), explain once, then declined | R42 human: ask once, then general | R43 scam line.
Back half (dates.json, multi.json): R16-17 get_account, then the debt statement | R18 bands early 1-7: 12 business days, 1 attempt; late 8-45: 7 business days, 2 attempts | R19-23 date table and clamping in code; next weekday = following_week | R24-25,27 amounts and floor (minimum_payment, instalment_amount) | R28 "Can you make this payment today?" | R30-31 ladder.
Ladder state machine: today -> refuse -> A1 full on end_of_week -> (refuse: late band -> A2 floor on deadline / early band -> refused) ; too_much -> SPLIT (once; half rounded up to the cent on end_of_week, rest on the deadline: a business day after instalment 1 and within deadline+10 business days) -> accept: read both back, one "Do you confirm?", create_promise instalment 1 then 2 (two tries each) ; refuse/too_much -> FLOOR_B (floor on end_of_week, the rest stays overdue) -> refuse: late -> A2 (floor on deadline) / early -> refused.
R32 hardship -> offer support, transferred/support or no_promise/hardship | R33 no other terms | R34-38 read-back, record, text-message sentence in the promise farewell | R39 paid | R40 dispute | R41 new plan (three or more parts) -> restructuring | R45 no_talk twice.
Closing: R46-47 one close_call; farewells in the closes; no add_note (notes: false).
Choices: the split dates are fixed by the system (end of week and deadline); if the customer gives their own split dates, the agent restates the offered dates (simplification of rule 26a). A customer asking for "two parts" relies on the model and the reply classification (no dedicated route).
